About the tunnel

Leake Street runs about 300 metres under the platforms of Waterloo station, from York Road towards Lower Marsh. Since Banksy's Cans Festival in May 2008 its walls have been a place where painting is allowed, and the work gets painted over constantly.

This page is a walk through it seen through a fisheye lens. Scroll to walk. Turn your head, or move your mouse, to read the walls: the history is on the left, the arches are on the right.

What's real

  • Length, the side arches, lamps, cable trays and drainpipes come from a SketchUp model built for this page. Width and height are estimates.
  • Three arches carry photos taken on site. The wall art in them belongs to the painters of Leake Street.
  • Every other piece of graffiti is generated fresh in your browser. Trains overhead are simulated.
  • Weather is live from Open-Meteo when this view can reach it.
